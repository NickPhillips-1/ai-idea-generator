# FOUNDERS LAB — Founder Interest & Opportunity Discovery


## BEFORE YOU START

This questionnaire finds what each founder would genuinely be excited to investigate and build.
It identifies directions to research, not proven customer demand.
Total time: about 75–90 minutes. Split it across two sittings if you like.
Step 1 – Main questionnaire: 45–60 min
Step 2 – Digital interest scan: 10–15 min
Step 3 – AI discovery report (optional): 10–15 min
Step 4 – Wrap-up: top picks and practical context: 10 min
Do the steps in this order. Answer in your own words before you see what your feed or an AI
says about you, so outside suggestions don't shape your answers.

### Ground rules

- Answer alone, before discussing anything with the other founders.
- Bullet points and fragments are fine. Specific beats polished: "watched 4 hours of videos on
home battery systems" beats "I like energy."
- Include ideas that seem impractical or that you couldn't build yourself.
- Skip any question you don't want to answer.
- Only list interests you recognize as genuine. Seeing a topic often doesn't mean you care about
it.
- Your raw answers are seen only by Founders Lab. Your cofounders see the summary you
approve.
- Deadline: [get er done]

## WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Follow-up questions. Founders Lab may send you up to 5 short follow-up questions. They'll
cover interests you mentioned but didn't explain, surprising combinations, gaps between what
you enjoy and what you'd build a business around, and spots where a specific example would
help. They won't repeat anything you've already answered clearly.
How your answers are used. For each founder, Founders Lab will:

1. List genuine interests in your own words.

2. Tag each as demonstrated (you spent real time or money on it), self-reported, or aspirational.

3. Pull out the technologies, industries, problems and customer groups you care about.

4. Suggest possible business and product directions.
Across all three founders, it will then flag:
- Interests shared by at least two founders
- Unexpected connections worth exploring
- Gaps, uncertainty and what needs more research
You stay in control. You review and approve your summary before your cofounders see it. A
direction can move forward when two founders are genuinely interested, even if neither has
expertise in it yet.
The goal isn't to find what we can already build. It's to find what we'd be excited to investigate,
invent and turn into a real business.
